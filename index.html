What Is Site Shades Industrial?

Site Shades Industrial delivers rugged, field-tested heat stress protection for roadside construction, surveying crews, utility linemen, and job sites. Designed to meet strict workplace heat illness prevention requirements, these shades mount directly to service trucks, equipment trailers, or standalone rigs to create immediate shaded cooling zones.
