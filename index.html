What Is Site Shades Sports?

Site Shades Sports was engineered specifically for youth and adult tournament athletics—including baseball, softball, soccer, and track. Designed to mount directly to chain-link dugouts and spectator bleachers in less than 5 minutes, our UV-rated breathable mesh keeps athletes cool between innings and spectators protected from blistering weekend sun.
